Kuan-Hsun Tu
National Taiwan University · Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering

I’m an undergraduate student advised by Prof. Tsung-Wei Ke in the Embodied AI lab. My research focuses on embodied AI that learns by exploration — building agents that form hypotheses about the world, test them through interaction, and update their beliefs to bridge the gap between semantic knowledge and physical reality.

Beyond research, I keep NTU CSIE’s infrastructure running as the department’s System Administrator and Lead TA with Prof. Hsin-Mu (Michael) Tsai, and partner with Prof. Chun-Yi Lee on high-performance computing initiatives such as Student Cluster Competition at SC25.
